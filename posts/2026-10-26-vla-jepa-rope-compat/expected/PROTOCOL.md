# VLA-JEPA rotary compatibility: protocol

Status: **FILLED, frozen 2026-10-25**, before any fixed-rotary number on real data exists. Owner-approved (written in
chat with Claude, committed by Ravindu). **Claude Code: do not edit this file.** BRIEF.md step 4 may run once step 2's
`CHECKS.md` is committed. Anything decided after seeing numbers is labelled exploratory.

## Decision

- **DEGRADES or COLLAPSES on M1:** my own future fine-tunes from a legacy checkpoint pin the legacy rotary (patch or a
  lerobot commit before `f9e9d73`) and say so in the model card; `UPSTREAM_DRAFT.md` proposes an opt-in flag
  `world_model_rope_legacy: bool` (default False), as upstream V-JEPA 2 kept for its released checkpoints.
- **SAFE on M1 (and M2 if run):** no pin; `UPSTREAM_DRAFT.md` gives the numbers as evidence and proposes one doc line
  saying continued training from legacy checkpoints is unaffected at this measurement.
- **NOT PROVEN:** numbers reported, no pin, no upstream suggestion beyond the measurement itself.

## Claim, translated

"On the same inputs, M1's legacy-trained world predictor has a ≥ 10% higher world-model L1 loss under the fixed rotary
(lerobot `d3793b7`) than under the legacy rotary." Upstream would test on its pretraining data (DROID); here the data is
each checkpoint's own training distribution, so this is a compatibility test (does the predictor still do what it
learned), not a generalisation test. The report says so.

## Metric

- Per window: the L1 loss exactly as `VLAJEPAModel._world_model_loss` computes it (mean over tokens), for `legacy`,
  `fixed` and `copy_last`, on the same encoder/Qwen tensors (BRIEF step 2 structure).
- Per episode: mean over its windows.
- Primary statistic: **R = mean over episodes of `fixed` / mean over episodes of `legacy`**, with a paired bootstrap over
  episodes (2 000 resamples, seed 0, windows of one episode kept together). Also the paired differences
  `fixed − legacy`, `fixed − copy_last`, `legacy − copy_last` with their 95% CIs.

## Sample

- **M1:** the 25 val episodes listed in `projects/vla-jepa-world-model/wm_train/split.json`
  (`ravediamond/so101_bowls_all_trimmed`); 8 windows per episode, start frames drawn uniformly over the valid range with
  `numpy.random.default_rng(0)` per episode index → 200 windows.
- **M2 (only if BRIEF step 1.4 allowed the download):** the first 20 LIBERO-10 episodes by episode index in
  `HuggingFaceVLA/libero`, same window rule → 160 windows.
- **Cost rule (fixed now):** if `CHECKS.md`'s seconds per window × windows > 2 h for a model, use 4 windows per episode
  for that model instead of 8. No other change is allowed.

## Baseline

`copy_last` (predict each next temporal position with the current one, same token alignment as the loss). It is the
cheapest competitor and is strong when the scene barely changes between positions; that is why the bar requires the
legacy predictor to beat it before any claim.

## Bar (per model)

1. **Instrument:** `legacy` beats `copy_last`: the 95% CI of `legacy − copy_last` is entirely below 0. If not:
   **NOT PROVEN** for that model (the setup cannot separate the effect), whatever R is.
2. **COLLAPSES:** instrument holds and the 95% CI of `fixed − copy_last` includes 0 or is above 0 (the fixed rotary
   brings the predictor down to "learned nothing usable").
3. **DEGRADES:** instrument holds, not COLLAPSES, R ≥ 1.10 and the 95% CI of `fixed − legacy` is entirely above 0.
4. **SAFE:** instrument holds and the 95% CI of R lies within [0.95, 1.05].
5. **NOT PROVEN:** anything else.

The verdict is worded exactly as one of these five labels.

## Exploratory (decided now, labelled as such later)

- Fraction of episodes where `fixed` is worse than `legacy`.
- Loss by predicted temporal position (does the gap grow with distance from the context).
- Loss by window position in the episode (start, middle, end thirds).
- M2 vs M1 contrast, if M2 runs.
